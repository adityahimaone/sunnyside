Vitrine v2.5 mark brainstorm

The mark draws the case. It never draws the light.

The page promises “a glass case for a low-resolution light”, then shows a solid, symmetric U with an underline. The system’s signature — hard glass against a pixel bloom — is absent from the one asset that has to carry it. The fix is not a new ornament on the U. The mark has to put both materials in one drawing. Six directions follow, all on the 64 grid, fill-only, constructed track, two to three primitives, one Signal element. Judge 16px first.

  • Empty, not litThe hero is a dithered bloom inside glass. The mark is an empty bucket: it shows the container and withholds the object.
  • Heaviest thing on the pageEvery other surface is a 1px edge and translucency. The mark is an 8-unit monolith with no interior, offset or light.
  • Reads as a letterUniform walls plus a bar at the baseline = a U with an underline, or a USB port. Neither is a vitrine.
  • No systemThe label is a 5-unit speck that dies under 32px. The compact is the same U with fatter walls, not a second drawing.

Shortlist, in a lockup

The mark has to hold beside the outlined wordmark at 30px and still say “glass + light” without the label copy. Three survive the 16px read.

01 · Half-light V — adopted

It is the only option that is bold at 128, clean at 32, legible at 16, and belongs to the wordmark rather than sitting next to it. It uses the system’s two materials as the idea, not as decoration: the ink arm is the glass, the Signal arm is the light. Keep the 6-unit plinth cut as the next refinement so it reads as a custom letterform, not a two-tone font sample.

02 · Vitrine in perspective — bold option

The only mark with depth, which is the system’s actual subject. It is also the riskiest: a box is a box. Develop it only if you want the case to be unmistakable; it needs a stepped edge and an asymmetric wall before it stops reading as a shipping logo.

03 · Corner light — abstract option

The most “design system” silhouette: a square with the dither eating one corner. It needs varied step sizes to read as dot gain instead of stairs, and the Signal corner is small at 16px. A good direction if the brand should stay abstract rather than lettered.

What happens after you pick

1 · Draw the compact

A separate drawing with fewer shapes and a heavier cut, tested at 16px on paper and ink. No scaled-down primary.

2 · Cut the wordmark

Freeze Space Grotesk and make one custom cut so the name and the mark share a construction, not just a colour.

3 · Build the cast

Four to six marks from the vitrine’s world — glint, label, dither dot, bracket — one cut line, one fill, one job each.

4 · Compose the marks section

Logo card, wordmark scale, cast drawer and one usage rule. The current section is a construction card, not a mark system.

5 · Rebuild the assets

Mark, compact, glyph, reverse, mono, lockups, favicons, board, tokens. One source, one QA run.

6 · Verify

16px on light and dark chrome, 390 and 1440, contrast pairs, one qa.mjs pass, gaps named.
