# Strand — brand brief

## Job

Give the portfolio its seventh design system (Case 07): a serene, minimalist coastal interface system built around white dune sand, marine basalt stone, tidal aquamarine, and a sweeping atmospheric coastal vista framing both hero and footer.

## Audience

Design engineers, creative directors, and product teams building contemplative, high-craft, minimalist interfaces: luxury architectural studios, high-end design tooling, editorial essays, and quiet digital retreats.

## Promise

**Where the dunes meet the tide.** A quiet, unhurried interface that lets content breathe.

## Positioning

Not a saturated summer beach resort with bright yellow umbrellas and cartoon surfboards. Not a dark crypto terminal. Not an AI gradient blur. Strand is an **architectural coastal study**: calm white sand plates, high-contrast marine basalt ink, a whisper of marram grass straw, and an authentic tidal vista grounding the top and bottom of the journey.

## Domain before defaults

The product's world is a coastal strand: wind-sculpted sand dunes sloping down to an empty ocean beach.

- **Five concepts:** the dune ridge, the wind ripple, the marram grass root, the tidal wash, the offshore horizon.
- **Five colours from the physical world:** dune linen, quartz sand, marine basalt, coastal haze, tidal aquamarine.
- **Signature:** an architectural S-monogram carved with a dune crest counter-form and a single aquamarine tidal wedge.
- **Obvious defaults to avoid:** cartoon sun emojis, palm tree icons, oversaturated cyan/yellow beach gradients, loud button hover glows, busy card grids.

## Constraints

- Stack: Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, CSS modules; no heavy component runtime.
- Gate: Sunnyside Brand System v2.5 constructed gate (zero circle, fill-only, 2 primitives in primary, 16px contrast verified).
- Sizes: mark at 16, 32, 48, 64, 180, 192, 512, 1024.
- Accessibility: WCAG AAA body contrast (≥ 14:1), WCAG AA secondary text (≥ 4.5:1), 44px touch targets.
- Hero & Footer: both framed by the real coastal vista photograph (`strand-vista.webp`).
