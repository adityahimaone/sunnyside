# Huginn — identity and system guide

> A picture becomes cells. A name becomes cells.

Huginn is Case 03 of the design-system library: a print-first identity for a
fictional two-city software studio (Oslo · Reykjavík). One bone sheet, one
vermilion field, and a single operation — the cell grid — carried at three
scales: the painting, the dissolve between painting and field, and the wordmark.

## Idea and mark

The identity depends on a drawing, not on a font. That is both the promise and
the constraint that produced the look: no webfont to load, no licence to buy, no
platform where the wordmark falls back to something else.

The **H is missing a cell**. It sits one half-cell to the right of the stem, in
the air, still flying. The omission is the whole logo — it is why the mark reads
at 16px, and it is why the mark is never redrawn or cleaned up.

- Primary: 65 × 50, stray cell included. Clear space is one cell on every side.
- Compact: 30 × 30, single glyph, stray cell dropped — below 16px the flying
  cell is noise.
- Wordmark: 950 × 200, 95 columns, 760 cells, one path. Never live text.
- Favicons are generated from the primary mark, not redrawn: 16, 32, 180, 192,
  512, plus a 32px SVG for the case route.

## Palette and type

Tokens are DTCG JSON, authored upstream and copied in — see
`public/brand/huginn/huginn-tokens.json`. Nothing here is generated by a script
in this repo; the exports are the source of truth for this copy.

| Role | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sheet (page ground) | `--surface` → `--hug-bone-200` | `#EBE5D8` |
| Chalk (raised) | `--surface-raised` → `--hug-bone-100` | `#F4EFE5` |
| Ink (body, edges) | `--ink` → `--hug-ink-900` | `#100F0E` |
| The loud field | `--accent-field` → `--hug-vermilion-500` | `#E2281B` |
| Accent (small type) | `--accent` → `--hug-vermilion-700` | `#A31810` |
| Focus ring | `--focus` → `--hug-cobalt-500` | `#1B2DB0` |

Sixteen primitives, sixteen semantic roles, ten space steps, seven type steps,
five durations. Components read roles, never primitives.

Three voices, none of them a webfont: `--font-reading` (a system serif),
`--font-label` (a system mono), and `--font-sheet: none` — because the wordmark
is a drawing, not a face. The vermilion field is for display and fills only:
12px text never sits on it, because at that size it measures 3.67:1 on the
sheet. Small text takes `--accent` instead, at 6.21:1.

## Material and motion

Ordered (Bayer) dither is the only signature effect, and it is applied as a
**mask**, not as a filter: a canvas is punched with transparent holes and laid
over the painting. No image pixel is ever read, so the effect survives `file://`,
a CDN, and a canvas-taint policy unchanged. It is a repeatable print boundary,
not a texture placed over every surface.

Grain is restrained print finish, not a second effect. Motion is entrance-only;
nothing loops. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the canvases paint their completed
state and every frame is byte-identical 500ms apart.

## Components and forms

The specimen documents the Wild Hunt Footer — a configurable footer with the
dissolve, the drawn wordmark, a calligraphic flourish and a reticle cursor — plus
a native form kit: text, email, select, textarea, checkbox, radio, switch and
submit, each with visible label, focus, invalid, disabled and success states.

The route is a specimen, not a published component library. The footer is **not**
the portfolio's global footer and must not be attached to the site shell.

## Do / don't

**Do** use the red as a single field, keep body text on opaque surfaces,
attribute the painting, test the mark at 16px, and keep the stray cell.

**Don't** add a raven mascot, distribute runes as decoration, place small copy on
the painting, use colour as the only state cue, or clean up the missing cell.

## Audit

Measured, not asserted. 43 targets × 2 themes with rendered-element contrast
(ancestor backgrounds composited, group opacity folded); 53 focusables all
ringed; reduced motion verified by frame comparison. The audit table in the
specimen also lists the two findings the first build got wrong and how they were
fixed. Full detail: `public/design-system/huginn/audit.md`.

## Gaps

The audit covers the Chromium build on this machine only; no other engine is
claimed. The painting is public domain (Peter Nicolai Arbo, *Åsgårdsreien*, 1872,
Nationalmuseum Oslo, via Wikimedia Commons) and its attribution travels with
every copy. No generated imagery and no font asset is part of this identity.
