# The craft bar: from a mark to a mark system

A good mark is not yet a brand. Case 01 (Sunnyside) is the quality bar because it
ships a **mark system**: a primary mark, a compact drawing, a hand-drawn
wordmark, a cast of supporting marks, and a composition that stages them with
clear space, specs, icons, downloads and one usage rule.

The v2.3 constructed track is still right for corporate and technical brands.
The mistake is using it for every brief. Choose a track before drawing.

## 1. Choose a track

| Track | Use when | Gate | What ships |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Constructed** | The brand is corporate, technical, architectural or infrastructure. No character is needed. | v2.3: zero-circle, fill-only, 2–4 primitives, one accent, five families. | Primary + compact + glyph + outlined wordmark + lockups + tokens + forms. |
| **Drawn** | The brand is editorial, warm, human, cultural, playful or object-led. A character or a hand is the point. | v2.4: one subject, one cut line, one flat fill, one hard offset, 2–3 inks, 16px legible, no gradients, no clipart, no live text. | Primary + compact + glyph + **drawn wordmark** + **cast of 4–6 marks** + composition + tokens + forms. |

The two tracks do not mix. A constructed ribbon with a smiley is two brands. A
drawn sun with a fill-only geometric counter is a third.

### The drawn gate (v2.4)

The v2.3 circle ban exists to stop Venn diagrams, orbit badges and concentric
rings. It is not a ban on the subject being round. In the drawn track:

- a circle is allowed **only when it is the subject** — a sun, a lens, a face, a
  record — never a container for an unrelated letter or a generic badge;
- one **cut line** (usually 2.6–3.4 units on the 64 grid) outlines the family;
- one **flat fill** per mark, plus at most one small accent detail;
- one **hard offset** belongs to the material; no blur, glow or gradient;
- at most **three inks** in the primary mark;
- the compact is a **separate drawing** with fewer details, not a scale-down;
- the mark still has a distinct outer silhouette from every other brand in the
  showcase;
- a face is allowed only when the voice is playful and the face survives 16px.

If a drawn mark cannot be described without a face or an ornament, it is a
constructed mark; use the v2.3 gate instead.

## 2. The mark system

Every expressive identity ships six pieces. Corporate identities may skip the
cast; they still need the first four and the composition.

1. **Primary mark.** One idea on the 64 grid. 1–3 fills, one cut line, one hard
   offset. Works in one colour first. At most one accent detail.
2. **Compact mark.** A separate drawing, fewer shapes, heavier cut, no fine
   detail. Test at 16px on paper and on ink before anything else.
3. **Glyph.** The mark without its tile or field, for pattern, stickers and
   large surfaces. It is not the favicon.
4. **Wordmark.** A drawing, never live text. Either a hand/script frozen as
   outlines, or a typeface outlined with one custom cut. See §5.
5. **Cast.** 4–6 supporting marks, drawn in the same cut line and flat fill.
   Each has one job: welcome, aside, celebration, energy, keep, point. See §4.
6. **Composition.** A marks section, board panel or case block that stages the
   system with a logo card, wordmark scale, cast drawer and one usage rule. See
   §3.

If the brief is a single mark, the primary and compact are enough. If the brief
is a brand kit or a design system, the wordmark and composition are not optional.

## 3. The composition (the Case 01 pattern)

The marks section is an essay, not a contact sheet. It has four movements:

1. **Logo card.** A dark stage, a clear-space box, the lockup, a rail label
   (`Logo / 01 · mark + wordmark`), then specs: header size, minimums, clear
   space, favicon sizes. Icons at 48/32/16. Two downloads. Two rules (do / don't).
2. **Wordmark card.** One large specimen, a dashed clear-space box, a scale row
   (40 / 24 / 16), and a sentence about when the hand is allowed and when the
   reading face takes over.
3. **Cast drawer.** 4–6 tiles in a 12-column grid. Each tile: the mark at
   64–96px, its name, its one job. The spans are intentionally uneven
   (4 / 5 / 4 / 3 / …), so the drawer reads as a collection, not a table.
4. **Usage rule.** One paragraph, in the brand voice: the cast is punctuation;
   never structure, never the only place a fact lives, never over type; 4–6 per
   composition is plenty.

The rails, the stage, the specs and the rule are the difference between a
sticker folder and a brand system. A page with five logo variants and no
wordmark, cast or rule fails the bar.

### Composition tokens

Name these once and reuse them on the board and the page:

| Token | Job |
| --- | --- |
| `--stage` | the dark or loud field for the logo stage |
| `--paper` | the card ground; the mark sits on paper, not on the stage |
| `--rail` | the 1px rule under the card label |
| `--offset` | the hard card/paper offset (0 if the material is flat) |
| `--drawer-span` | the 12-column cast spans (`4 5 4 3`) |
| `--mark-size` | 64 primary, 16 compact, 96 cast |

The cards use the same radius, edge and offset as the rest of the system. If the
material is glass, the stage is glass and the cards are solid; if the material is
paper, the cards are paper and the offset is hard ink.

## 4. The cast

A cast is not a second logo set. It is **punctuation**: marks that carry a
feeling or a small action, never structure and never a fact.

### Rules

- 4–6 marks. More is a clip-art folder.
- One cut line, one flat fill, one hard offset. The cast shares the primary's
  material, not just its palette.
- Each mark has a written job. If you cannot write the job in one line, delete
  it.
- One accent colour maximum across the cast; the fill can alternate between two
  inks only if the cut line stays identical.
- No gradients, glows, raster images, emoji or stock icons.
- The cast is `aria-hidden` in the page. It never carries meaning alone.
- Never put the cast over type, in a form field, or in a favicon.
- The cast is optional for constructed/corporate brands. Skip it rather than
  invent a cute mascot.

### Vocabulary

Derive the cast from the product's **physical world**, not from a generic
sticker pack. Choose 4–6 from one family:

| Family | Shapes | Reads as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Celestial | sun, moon, star, eclipse | warmth, cycle, night, focus |
| Studio | spark, burst, arc, daisy, smiley | celebration, energy, welcome |
| Object | bolt, heart, drop, leaf, eye, key | speed, care, material, attention, access |
| Editorial | asterisk, pilcrow, rule, bracket, arrow | annotation, quotation, direction, section |
| Signal | dot, wave, chevron, node, pulse | data, transmission, movement, system |

The Case 01 cast — sun, smiley, daisy, burst, heart, bolt — works because the
brand is a warm studio wall. A database product using the same set would be
lying. Copy the principle, not the sun.

### Construction

- 64 grid; 3–3.4 cut line; round caps; 2–4 fill paths.
- Draw the silhouette first at 24px; if it is a smudge, simplify.
- A face: bar eyes, an arc smile, cheeks at 55% opacity; nothing smaller than
  2.4 units at 16px.
- A burst: 8–12 rays from one centre, clipped by a field if the material crops.
- A daisy: 6–8 petals, one centre; keep the petal gap ≥ 5 units.
- A heart/leaf/drop: one closed path, one cut line, one optional vein.
- Test the cast at 24px, 48px and 96px. It does not need a 16px favicon.

Use `scripts/cast-forge.mjs` for a drawn starting set, then edit the paths. The
script is a draft, not a taste test.

## 5. The wordmark

The wordmark is the second half of the identity, and the cheapest place to look
generic.

- **Drawn route.** Use the brand's own hand, or draw a single-stroke script:
  one continuous path per word, 3.3 stroke, round caps, `skewX(-9)` if the hand
  leans, plus one dot/extra path. Freeze it. Case 01 uses a 116.15 × 33.1
  viewBox and one hand across the name, the footer and the section annotations.
- **Type route.** Choose one face, instance the weight, shape real kerning,
  apply tracking in em, outline with `extract-wordmark.py`, then make one custom
  cut or join so it is not a typed name.
- **Never** use `<text>` in the final lockup. A font failure must not change the
  logo.
- Test at 16, 24, 40, 64 and 160px wide. The hand can be expressive at 160 and
  still legible at 24; if it cannot, the reading face carries the name at small
  sizes.
- Record the optical gap, the wordmark height and the name size. Do not align
  the mark and wordmark on a geometric baseline; align them optically.

A brand with a drawn mark but an outlined system font is half-finished. The
wordmark is where the hand becomes ownable.

## 7. What Vitrine still misses

Vitrine's v2.3 folded V is a strong constructed mark. Its marks section is still
a construction card plus a gate, not a mark system. It has no drawn wordmark, no
cast, and no logo card / wordmark scale / drawer / rule. The next pass should
add:

- a cast derived from glass and dither — a lens arc, a dot, a signal burst, a
  bracket, a glare, a registration mark — each with a job;
- a drawn or custom-cut wordmark that belongs to the folded V;
- the four-movement composition in §3.

That is the gap between “a good mark” and “the Case 01 bar.”

## 8. Tools

- `templates/marks-section.html` — the four-movement composition, copyable into
  a page or board.
- `scripts/cast-forge.mjs` — 4–6 cast marks from one config; one cut line, one
  fill, one job each. `--list` shows the vocabulary; `--init` starts a config.
  Add `"cast": "public/brand/<slug>/cast"` to `audit.json` on the drawn track;
  `audit.mjs` checks the 64 grid, the cut line, live text and the 4–6 count.
- `examples/configs/sunnyside-cast.json` — the Case 01 cast, as a worked config.
- `templates/design-system.html` and `templates/brandkit.html` — the marks
  section and the cast panel are already wired into the starters.
- `scripts/qa.mjs` — one command for audit + score + browser.

## 9. Anti-slop

- Five logo cards and no wordmark, cast or usage rule.
- A mark in a tile with no clear-space diagram, specs or minimums.
- A cast of 12 cute shapes with no jobs.
- A face on a brand that has no warmth.
- A circle used as a container for an unrelated letter.
- Gradients, glows, drop shadows that are not hard, and raster logos.
- Mixing cut-line weights across the family.
- A drawn mark that does not share a material with its cast.
- A wordmark that is live text, or a typed name with no custom cut.
- A marks section that is a grid of equal tiles with no hierarchy.

## 10. Acceptance

- [ ] The track is named (constructed or drawn) and the gate matches it.
- [ ] The primary mark reads at 32px; the compact reads at 16px on paper and ink.
- [ ] A glyph exists; it is not used as the favicon.
- [ ] The wordmark is outlined or drawn; no `<text>`.
- [ ] The wordmark has a clear space, a scale row and a minimum.
- [ ] The cast has 4–6 marks; each has a one-line job; one cut line; one fill.
- [ ] The cast is `aria-hidden` and never carries a fact.
- [ ] The composition has a logo card, wordmark card, cast drawer and usage rule.
- [ ] The logo card has specs, icons, downloads and do/don't.
- [ ] The board and the page show the same composition at 1440 and 390.
- [ ] The guide says what the cast is for and when to skip it.
- [ ] The audit names the gaps.
