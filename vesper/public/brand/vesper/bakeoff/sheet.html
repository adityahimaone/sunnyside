Lodestar

The star that stayed.

One object, two readings: a four-point star at speed, a lamppost at rest. The lower point is the post — nothing is added to the star, something is taken from it.

Risk A four-point star is the most-used mark shape of the decade; only the post separates it from the sparkle.
