# Porphyry — story

**Belief.** A screen can be composed like a stage: a ground, a light, and figures that stand
on their own depth.

**Enemy.** The template. Flat cards on a grey field, depth faked with a drop shadow, light
faked with a gradient, a logo made of a typed initial in a rounded square.

**The mark as argument.** A stage opening cut into a block of porphyry. The stone is the
frame; the trapezoid opening is the stage; the 8-unit gold threshold is the only light. Two
filled shapes, zero strokes, and a top-right corner cut as it came out of the quarry. It is
not a letter; the wordmark already carries the name.

**Material.** Porphyry is an imperial stone — deep, red, and not a neutral. Marble is the lit
ink. Gold is punctuation. The stage is near-black but never pure black; it keeps a red shadow.

**Voice.** Declarative, concrete, a little severe. It names the material and the decision. It
does not say "unleash" or "elevate". A sentence can be rejected against it:
*"A page composed like a stage: one stone, one light, and a cast that stands in front of the
word."*

**Proof.** The loader fills the word with marble; three photographs are keyed off their own
grounds; the cast rises in front of the word; the whole sequence collapses to its end state
under reduced motion. The page is readable without the stage at all.

**Gap.** The two-ink grade is composited in software. No gold leaf was applied, and no press
proof exists. The cast is stock photography, not a commissioned shoot. The stage is an
ornament above a static document.

## Seven beats

1. **The belief.** A screen can be composed like a stage.
2. **The enemy.** The flat template, the fake shadow, the gradient pretending to be light.
3. **The word.** The wordmark is the back wall: outline, marble fill, then the room.
4. **The cast.** Photographs of marble keyed by their own luminance, on their own depth.
5. **The material.** Porphyry, marble, gold leaf; one loud field; zero radius.
6. **The rule.** One light, one effect, one accent. Depth is an event in time.
7. **The gap.** Screen, not press. Stock, not commission. Named, not hidden.

## Why the rest lost

- **The old gilded P.** A typed P in a frame. The gold bowl was doing all the work; the
  wordmark already carried the letter.
- **The meander ring.** The component's best ornament and the worst logo: a badge, an orbit,
  a stamp. It stays in the loader.
- **The curtain.** Two stone slabs and a gold seam. At 16px the seam and the gap merge into a
  pause icon.
- **The capital.** Abacus, gold shadow gap, shaft, plinth. It reads as a T, or a table, before
  it reads as a column.
- **The arch P.** A Didone P with a gold foot. Better than the old P, but still a letter.
- **The gold doorway.** The full-gold opening was the strongest 16px mark and the weakest
  identity: the stone became a frame around a gold wedge.

The decision cards are on the case page and in `porphyry-data.ts`.
