Doop & Figma Import Helper
Figma restricts single frames to max 10,000px on each side.
Why does Doop show "Frames must be at most 10,000px"?
Figma has a strict internal limit: no frame can exceed 10,000px. When all 8 sheets are stacked vertically, the page totals ~13,500px. Choose one of the fixes below to import cleanly:

Option A: Doop Clean — single column (recommended)

One 1440px column, no grid, no sticky bar, no shadows or transforms. This is the layout an importer should snapshot — it removes the extra layers and clipped frames that make the imported page look messy.
