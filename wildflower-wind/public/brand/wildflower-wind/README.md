reason a resized SVG is not a re-struck mark.

## The petal

Narrow at the base, widest at 72% of its length, rounded at the tip, bent by 8–16% of
that length. Let the width fall to zero at the tip and the same curve becomes a leaf,
which is the wrong plant; the rounded cap is what keeps it a flower. `scripts/petal.mjs`
holds the spine, the width profile and the cap.
