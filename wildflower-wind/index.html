Wildflower Wind

A seed house sells a future it cannot photograph. So the identity does not show the flower. It shows the flower through glass: four petals still holding, one already gone, and the crossing where they overlap gone dark.

Primitives · 9Pairs computed · 15 Published as failing · 2Mark files · 15
01Premise

Nothing here sits on top of anything.

A pane of glass does not hide what is behind it, and it does not leave it alone either. It bends it, softens it, and catches one edge of light. That is the whole interface: a meadow that stays visible, and surfaces you read through rather than on.

The mark is built the same way. Five translucent petals are laid down, and where two cross they multiply — so the flower has no outline and no ink of its own. It has a dark crossing and a lit rim, and the fifth petal is the one the wind already took.

Belief
Arrangement is the enemy of a meadow. A good page looks gathered, not composed.
Enemy
Two defaults: the symmetrical bouquet with a script logotype, and the opaque white card that hides whatever is behind it. One is pretty. The other is furniture.
Material
Frosted glass over a meadow. Every surface is translucent, every edge catches light on one side only, and whatever is behind stays visible through it.
Test
Reject the work if it could be flattened onto white without losing anything.
01

The mark is glass, so the overlap is the ink.

Because
Five translucent petals multiply where they cross. The darkest thing in the mark is the crossing, not a stroke — which is the whole argument in one rule.
Not when
The surface prints in one colour. Then the petals go solid and the crossings are cut out instead.
Breaks next
Giving a petal a solid fill. The glass becomes a sticker.
02

Every surface is a pane, and a pane has one lit edge.

Because
Light comes from the top-left of the field. A border on all four sides is a card; one bright edge and one long shadow is glass.
Not when
The surface sits inside a photograph, where there is no page light — then use the scrim, not a border.
Breaks next
Adding a second shadow direction. Two lights make a drop shadow.
03

Radius is nested, never uniform.

Because
A 12px corner inside a 28px pane reads as a lens. The same 12px inside a 40px pane reads as a mistake — the inner radius has to be the outer minus the padding.
Not when
The element is smaller than twice the radius. Then it is a pill.
Breaks next
Rounding every corner to the same value. That is a card grid with soft edges.
04

The photograph is the light, not the background.

Because
The meadow is blurred to 34px behind everything. It exists so the glass has something to bend, and it never competes with a word.
Not when
The crop would put a horizon through the text. Move the pane, not the photograph.
Breaks next
Putting a sharp photograph inside a pane. Then it is a picture, not light.
02Calyx

Five petals. One gone.

A petal is narrow at the base, widest at 72% of its length, rounded at the tip, and bent by 8–16% of that length. Four hold the calyx at 74°, 72° and 80°; the fifth sits 134° away, small, further out, in the gap the wind opened. It is the only place the accent is allowed in the mark.

03Light

Nine values, and one of them is the light.

The haze is the air the page sits in, the pane is what you read through, and the ink is the darkest thing the glass resolves to. The violet is the cosmos seen through the pane — refraction is why it is not pink. Gold and sky are light, never words: on this ground they cannot reach 4.5:1, and pretending otherwise is how a palette becomes a brochure.

06Ledger

What is proved, and what is not.

Proved

  • Fifteen contrast pairs recomputed from hex at build time; the stated ratios are the computed ones.
  • Nine primitives in tokens.json match the token CSS at the same value, in both scopes.
  • The mark at 16px is a different drawing — four placements, not five.
  • The wordmark is outlines from Instrument Serif. No font file can change the name.

Not verified

  • Glass on a low-contrast display. Backdrop blur varies by GPU and browser; the pane is designed to hold without it.
  • The meadow photograph behind the page. It was supplied for this system and carries no licence record.
  • Print. A translucent mark on coated stock is a different problem from a translucent mark on a screen.
  • Figma parity. The plugin carries variables and styles, not the blend modes that make the petals glass.

Known gaps

  • gold on haze, 2.19:1 — graphic only. It may never carry a label, including a tin’s variety name.
  • sky on haze, 1.47:1 — surface only. Never a rule, never an icon that must be read.
  • Backdrop blur is unverified below Safari 16.4 and Firefox 103.
  • The cast has four members. A fifth — a petal that cracks — was drawn and dropped: it read as damage.
